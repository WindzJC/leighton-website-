Moon over Seaville

A wildfire story in four episodes by Dr. Leighton J. Reynolds

Eight wildfires in one October, and the evidence burns with them. So a psychoanalyst sets out to track the arsonist through the mind.

The four episodes

Episode 1

Seaville, California, north of Los Angeles

From the Other Side of the Moon

2020, 192 pages

What burns

Dr. Roger Sterling’s two children are trapped at home behind a fire line, their nanny lying motionless on the garage floor. The only way to them is a steep two-mile run uphill, into smoke, wind and fire.

What it reveals

A week earlier, with eight fires in a month and the evidence burned, Sterling told Fire Chief Richard Bradford his idea: a serial arsonist might be tracked through the dynamics of the mind.

Episode 2

Six months after the Canyon Fire

In Search of Aginsky’s Mind

2020, 294 pages

What burns

An arsonist still out there, a kidnapping, a hit man, stolen intellectual property and a firestorm, all inside twenty-four hours.

What it reveals

Synchronicity ties Seaville to Long Island and Central Tennessee, and everything turns on Aginsky’s mind: the rights to a theory of the universe’s ongoing evolution, and keys to what creates wildfires worldwide.

Episode 3

While Sterling searches for his kidnapped children

What’s Behind the Moon

2021, 408 pages

What burns

The entire Seaville Valley is engulfed in a huge firestorm, and the community struggles to survive its own destructive elements.

What it reveals

A wildfire breaks open seeds that have lain dormant for years. It does the same to a community’s secrets, for better or for worse.

Episode 4

The darker side of Seaville

The End?

2021, 478 pages

What burns

Who would want to burn down the entire community of Seaville, and for what? The discovery of Green Oil?

What it reveals

Whether there is any resolution to 13 vs. 5, to Eros vs. Thanatos, or to the evolutionary chess board.

Under the arson investigation runs the question the series keeps asking: is there any resolution to 13 vs. 5?

Thirteen for the forces of destruction, five for the forces of life. Eros against Thanatos. It’s a psychological, action, and erotic thriller, written by a psychoanalyst.

He watched the valley burn.

Leighton J. Reynolds moved to Santa Clarita in 2001. In October 2007 he watched three wildfires burn across the Santa Clarita Valley at once, and by March 2008 he had started writing. Seaville is modeled on that community.

He researched arson and spoke with L.A. County arson investigators. He holds a doctorate in Psychoanalytic Studies, is a Certified Psychoanalyst, and has worked as a neuro-psychoanalyst with patients living with PTSD and traumatic brain injury. He still lives and works in Southern California, in the heart of wildfire country.
